Privacy Policy

How Heychat handles your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Heychat collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use our app, website, marketplace, and related services.

1. Introduction

Heychat (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides a communication platform that enables users to send messages, share media, participate in group chats, access marketplace services through Heyshop, and use related features on our mobile application and official website at heychathq.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, and protect your personal data when you use Heychat, visit our website, interact with our services, or contact our support and partnerships teams.

We are committed to handling personal data responsibly and transparently. We collect only the information necessary to operate the service, maintain account security, support marketplace activity, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

By creating an account or using Heychat, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

A. Information you provide directly

We collect personal information that you provide when you:

  • create an account
  • verify your phone number with SMS OTP
  • complete your profile
  • contact us through email or support channels
  • use Heyshop as a seller or buyer
  • submit forms or requests on our website

This may include:

  • phone number
  • display name
  • profile photo
  • bio or status text
  • email address
  • business name and contact details
  • seller verification information
  • product listings and marketplace details
  • support messages and inquiry details

B. Content you transmit through the service

We process the content needed to deliver the Heychat experience, including:

  • private messages
  • group chat conversations
  • images
  • videos
  • audio notes
  • file attachments
  • status updates
  • translation requests

If real-time translation is enabled, the selected text may be temporarily processed to return a translated version in your preferred language. We do not use your private chats to train public AI models.

C. Automatically collected information

When you use Heychat or visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • device model and operating system
  • unique device identifiers
  • IP address
  • network provider
  • app version
  • browser type and language
  • crash reports and technical diagnostics
  • performance and latency data
  • push notification tokens
  • account activity and security logs

D. Website information

When you visit heychathq.com, we may also collect:

  • pages visited
  • date and time of access
  • referral source
  • browser information
  • device and network metadata
  • IP address
  • session-related information

E. Heyshop marketplace information

If you use Heyshop, we may collect:

  • business identity information
  • product information and listings
  • pricing and transaction details
  • buyer and seller communications
  • verification documents
  • payment and order-related records

3. How we use your information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • to create and manage your account
  • to verify your identity using SMS OTP
  • to deliver messaging, media sharing, and communication features
  • to enable and support Heyshop marketplace services
  • to send notifications and alerts
  • to improve product stability, functionality, and performance
  • to detect and prevent fraud, spam, and abuse
  • to respond to support requests and inquiries
  • to enforce our terms and community standards
  • to comply with applicable laws and legal requests

We use personal information only where it is necessary and proportionate to the purposes described above.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for marketing purposes.

We may share your data with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our platform, including:

  • Cloudflare for security and website infrastructure
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications
  • SMS verification services for OTP authentication
  • payment processors for marketplace transactions
  • customer support and communication tools
  • security and fraud prevention services

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or regulatory request, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Heychat, our users, or others.

5. Data storage and security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

  • encrypted transmission using secure protocols
  • restricted access to personal data
  • account-level security controls
  • monitoring and incident response procedures

6. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

  • account information while your account remains active
  • support records needed for customer care and compliance
  • transaction records needed for accounting and legal obligations
  • operational logs retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting

7. Your rights and account deletion

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data
  • request corrections to inaccurate information
  • request deletion of your account and related data
  • object to or restrict certain processing
  • request data portability where applicable

Delete your account

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  • Open Heychat
  • Go to Settings
  • Select Account
  • Choose Delete Account
  • Confirm your mobile number

If you no longer have access to the app, you may submit a deletion request through our website or by emailing support@heychathq.com with the subject line: “Account Deletion Request.”

8. Children’s privacy

Heychat is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age without valid parental consent or as required by applicable law.

If we become aware that a child has created an account without proper consent, we will take steps to remove the account and delete associated personal data where required by law.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in locations outside your home country, including countries where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction. We use trusted service providers and appropriate safeguards to support lawful transfer and processing of personal data.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, or business operations. When we make updates, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

For significant changes, we may notify users through in-app notices, email, or website announcements.

11. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:

  • Website: https://heychathq.com
  • Support: support@heychathq.com
  • Privacy: privacy@heychathq.com
  • Partnerships: partnerships@heychathq.com
